# Deliberate Pace Guiding

Website for Deliberate Pace Guiding, a hiking guide service offering small group day hikes in Appalachia and Southeastern Alaska.

Built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and deployed via GitHub Pages to [www.deliberatepaceguiding.com](https://www.deliberatepaceguiding.com).

## Alaska pricing — competitor comparison

Reference for how our Southeast Alaska guide rates compare to other Juneau operators.
Our tiers are private, small-group day hikes (4–6 hours): **$650** for 1–2 guests,
**$850** for 3, **$1,050** for a group of 4.

### Per-group price by operator

| Operator | Model | 1–2 guests | 4 guests | Transport included? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deliberate Pace (us)** | Small private | $650 | $1,050 | ❌ No |
| Tongass Treks | Small private | $450 | $700 | ✅ Yes |
| Beyond AK (Mendenhall) | Large operator | $1,672 private / $209 pp shared | $1,672 private | ✅ Yes |
| Beyond AK (West Glacier) | Large private | $1,194 (1–6, flat) | $1,194 (1–6, flat) | ✅ Yes |
| Step Into Alaska | Shared / cruise channel | Not published | Not published | ✅ Yes |

**Notes:**
- We are the only operator listed that does **not** include trailhead transport;
  add ~$70–90 round-trip taxi/rideshare for a true all-in comparison.
- Beyond AK and Step Into Alaska run longer, strenuous, fully-outfitted days
  (6–8 hours, van transport, permits, rain gear); not strictly apples-to-apples.
- We publish our prices openly; the cruise-channel operators quote by inquiry.

## Appalachia pricing — competitor comparison

Reference for our Appalachia guide rates (New River Gorge National Park and
Monongahela National Forest). Our trips are small-group day hikes (2–3 hours),
spring and fall, all levels, 1–6 guests.

### Our rates

| Group size | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 4 guests | ~$500 per trip | Base rate |
| 5–6 guests | ~$500 + small added fee | Larger groups welcome |
